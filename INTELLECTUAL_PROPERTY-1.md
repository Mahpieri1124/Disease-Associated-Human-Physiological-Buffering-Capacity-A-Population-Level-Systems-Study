## 3. Programme-Specific Construct

**Human Physiological Buffering Capacity** is treated in this programme as a provisional research construct describing the hypothesised capacity of an integrated human physiological state to absorb, regulate, and recover from perturbation.

It is not presented as an established medical diagnosis, validated biomarker, clinical score, or universally accepted scientific construct.

Applied Research Module 10 extends this construct to the study of disease-associated population-level burden.
